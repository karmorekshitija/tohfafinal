Celebrating the soul of Indian craftsmanship

In an era of mass production and identical goods, TOHFA champions the distinct beauty of handmade creations. From hand-poured soy candles and bespoke ceramic drinkware to artisanal jewellery and customized couple keepsakes, each item is crafted with intention and patience.

We bridge the distance between India’s talented artisans and buyers who value individuality, heritage, and genuine thoughtfulness. When you choose a gift on TOHFA, you directly support an independent maker's livelihood and keep traditional craft alive.
