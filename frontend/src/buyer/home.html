Refunds & Disputes

At Tohfa, every item is crafted with love and care by independent artisans across India. Because many pieces are custom-made or personalized, our policies protect both creators and buyers.

Custom & Bespoke Items

Customized products are non-refundable once production has begun, unless the item arrives damaged or materially different from approved specifications.

Standard Catalog Items
